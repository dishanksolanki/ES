What MedExpert Does

Four modules, one transparent pipeline

Each stage of the assessment is a distinct, inspectable module — designed so the reasoning behind a result is never a mystery.

Module 01

Symptom Assessment

Answer simple questions about your symptoms — no medical jargon required.

Module 02

Rule-Based Analysis

The system evaluates your answers using a structured medical knowledge base.

Module 03

Clear Explanation

Understand exactly which symptoms and rules contributed to the assessment.

Module 04

Health Guidance

Receive general information and suggested next steps to discuss with a professional.
